How to play

Turning

  • Drag a sticker to turn its layer that way.
  • Or use the buttons on the right, or the keys U D F B R L for the up, down, front, back, right and left faces. Hold Shift to turn counterclockwise.

Looking around

  • Drag around the cube to orbit it. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Double-click or double-tap to go back to the starting view.
  • The sun or moon button in the corner switches between the dark and light theme.

Moves and patterns

  • The strip of moves near the bottom shows every move you've made, the latest one highlighted. Tap a move to go back to just after it, or a later one to go forward again, like a video's timeline. Start goes back to the beginning.
  • The grid button in the corner (or P) opens the patterns: famous ones like the checkerboard and the superflip, played move by move from a solved cube.
  • Under them, How many repeats? repeats a short sequence, like R U, faster and faster until the cube is back where it started, counting as it goes. Escape stops it.

Saving and sharing

  • Your cube, its moves and the cube you're painting are remembered, even if you close the page.
  • The share button next to ? makes a link to your cube. Whoever opens it gets exactly the same cube, scramble included.

Buttons

  • Scramble mixes up the cube. Solve it yourself for a little celebration.
  • Undo (Ctrl Z) and Redo (Ctrl Shift Z) step through your moves. On a Mac, Cmd works too.
  • Reset rewinds every move back to the start.
  • Solve shows how to solve the cube with the beginner method, starting with the white cross: one move at a time, with the face to turn glowing. Step with Back and Next (or ← →), play and pause with Space, or drag the slider. Auto-solve plays the rest as a show, and Share sends the cube with its solution.
  • Color my cube lets you paint your real cube's colors into the app.

Notation

Faces are named by position: U up (red), D down (orange), F front (white), B back (yellow), R right (green) and L left (blue). A letter alone is a clockwise turn as seen looking at that face, R' is counterclockwise, and R2 is a half turn.
